# Offline FAW action reference photographs

These are real reference photographs, downloaded as publisher thumbnails. They are not generated images. No retouching was performed; the app displays them with `BoxFit.contain` and permits zooming. The originals remain subject to their respective licenses. Bundling a CC BY-SA image does not change the license of unrelated application code.

Retrieval date: 2026-10-07. Publisher metadata and download URLs are retained in `docs/FAW_ACTION_IMAGE_PROVENANCE.json`. The app exposes credits, source URLs and licenses through **Photo credit and guidance**, and credits the reference again in the zoom view.

The egg photograph is explicitly labelled magnified, not actual size. The frass-triggered inspection guide shows the larva to look for, and explicitly says its photograph does not depict frass. Leaf feeding marks alone do not establish species identity or current feeding.

The widget-preview fixture uses the larva reference as its simulated farmer photograph. Production comparison uses the farmer's saved evidence file, preferentially the photo associated with the relevant model detection.
